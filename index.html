GBIF.org Design System

GBIF.org is a data portal first: pages exist so people can find, judge and reuse biodiversity records. The interface stays quiet, white and grey, so that photographs, maps and numbers carry the page, and one green, the GBIF leaf green, marks everything you can act on.

Content fundamentals

  • Plain, factual, international English. Many readers are not native speakers. Short sentences, no idioms, British spelling ("programme", "digitized" as in the source records).
  • Name things by their GBIF type. Occurrence, dataset (occurrence / checklist), publisher, species, data use, project, programme, event, news, tool, document, page. Use these exact words in Pills, TypeLabels and SummaryNavCards.
  • Numbers are evidence. Always show the figure and the noun ("156 occurrences", "39 citations", "803,508,263 records in total"), with thousands separators. Never round or abbreviate without saying so.
  • Sentence case for headings, buttons and links ("Explore the gallery", "Show suggestions"). Caps only through the type styles that set them: subtitle-1, subtitle-2, label, overline, and Select options.
  • Credit and cite. Images are occurrence photos, so carry the licence or credit. Datasets show their DOI.
  • No emoji, no exclamation marks, no marketing superlatives.

Real copy from the designs: "Free and open access to biodiversity data", "71,510 occurrences with images", "This page might have existed once, but we can no longer find it. Please try our search to find what you're looking for."

Colour

  • Pages are white. Bands behind floating panels are surface-sunken. Quiet sections and contact details sit on paper.
  • Text is text (ink-80, 8.5:1 on white). Secondary meta lines are text-muted (ink-60, 4.3:1, keep to 14px+). Caps subtitles and placeholders are text-subtle (ink-50, 3.2:1, large or decorative only). Card titles and top-nav labels use ink-strong.
  • brand (green-100, #4C9C2E) is for actions, links, selected states and content-type labels, and nothing decorative. It is 3.44:1 on white, below 4.5:1 for small text. The XD values are kept exactly, so pair green text with an underline (links), an icon (Pill, TypeLabel, CountTag) or 16px bold (buttons).
  • brand-soft (green-10) is the only fill behind green text. green-stroke outlines green buttons and tags.
  • The green ramp's tints (green-90 … green-10) are for hover fills, selected rows and maps. The XD library has no 60% green step.
  • The other eight ramps (coral, pink, peach, cyan, periwinkle, purple, yellow, ocean, each 100 → 10) are categorical: chart series, map layers and data-type accents. They never mean success, warning or error. Of their 100 steps, only ocean-100 passes 4.5:1 for text on white.
  • One theme, light. The XD files define no dark mode.

Type

  • 2026 scale (current): Helvetica Neue for everything. Headings h1–h6 are Bold at 48 / 38 / 32 / 24 / 20 / 16. Body is body-1 (16/25.6) and body-2 (14/22.4). UI uses card-title, card-body, nav and label (all Medium).
  • subtitle-1 and subtitle-2 stay in Rubik SemiBold caps, as on both type boards. caption is Cormorant Medium Italic. overline is Roboto Medium 10, caps, letter-spaced.
  • 2022 scale (editorial): Bitter SemiBold headings (serif-h1–serif-h5) and Bitter Bold 16 news-card titles (serif-h6), with Rubik body. Keep it for news and editorial pages built from the 2022 boards. Do not mix Bitter and Helvetica headings on one page.
  • Helvetica Neue is a system font with no web file here. Bitter, Rubik, Roboto and Cormorant load from Google Fonts via components/bundle.css.

Space, shape, depth

  • Spacing is measured from the layouts: space-1 4, space-2 8, space-3 12, space-4 16, space-5 20, space-7 28, space-8 32, space-10 40. Result cards pad space-4, news cards space-5, and panels inset space-7.
  • Corners: radius-xs (2px) for tags, DOI, count tags, pagination and 2026 result cards. radius-sm (4px) for buttons, search bars, panels and news cards. radius-full for pills, chips, filter tabs and toggles. The filter popover is the one 16px exception.
  • Shadows are soft and short: shadow-card for result and summary cards, shadow-panel for menus and panels, shadow-raised only for the floating search bar, shadow-hover for a hovered news card, and shadow-header under the top bar.
  • Borders are 1px: border and border-subtle for dividers, and green outlines only on interactive or tag elements. The accent Divider is a 36×3 green rule under section titles.
  • Focus: a 2px solid focus-ring (brand green) with a 2px offset on every interactive element.

Imagery

Photographs of real occurrences, cropped full-bleed: card tops, 169×120 result thumbnails, wide post headers and the image gallery. Never stock photography or illustration in UI chrome. The 404 page is the exception, using a collage of historical scientific illustrations. Show the photographer credit when the image carries one.

Iconography

  • Content-type icons (Assets → Content icons, 13 glyphs) are the GBIF set from the XD icon sheet, at 40 / 20 / 16px in brand. Each one stands for a type: occurrences (pin), datasets (layers), species (tree), publishers (paper plane), news (megaphone), events (calendar), data use (document search), projects (rocket), programmes (orbit), tools, documents, resources and pages.
  • UI glyphs are Material Icons, as named in the XD layers: search, translate, chevron, expand_more, filter_list, format_quote, list, today, help, chat, check and info. close, more-vert, download and flag are not in the XD files, so the standard Material paths stand in for them.
  • In React use GBIF.Icon (currentColor, see GBIF.iconNames). The SVG files in Assets carry fixed inks for use in <img>.
  • No emoji.

Components

components/bundle.js defines window.GBIF, 35 React 18 components grouped as Actions, Tags & labels, Forms, Content, Navigation and Foundations. Each has a guideline (components/<Name>/README.md) and a live preview. Notes and component docs paraphrase the XD artboards. The files they came from are "GBIF-2026.xd" (styles, record pages) and "search - 2026.xd" (search, pills, icons, 404).

Not carried over from the XD: the dataset/occurrence/country page layouts themselves (they are compositions of these components), the image gallery and image-state overlays, donut/chart components, the mobile menu and mobile layouts, and dark or high-contrast themes (none are defined). Chip hover is #BFBFBF as drawn, and the popover radius (16px) is taken from its master.

Colour

Ten ramps from the XD document library, each from full strength (100) to a 10% tint, and the semantic roles built on them.

green

GBIF green — the brand hue. Step 100 is the primary action fill and link colour; lighter steps are tints for selected rows, pill backgrounds (green-10) and hover fills.

ink

Neutral ink scale (warm grey). 100 is near-black; 80 is the default text colour; 50 muted labels; 30–20 hairlines and borders; 10 sunken fills.

coral

Categorical hue for charts and data-type accents. Never a status colour on its own.

pink

Categorical hue for charts and content accents.

Categorical hue for charts and content accents.

Open preview ↗
